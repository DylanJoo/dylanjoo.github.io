Hi there! I'm Jia-Huei (Dylan) Ju, a PhD student at the [IRLab](https://irlab.science.uva.nl/), University of Amsterdam, supervised by [Dr. Andrew Yates](https://andrewyates.net/) since 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and neural information retrieval.

Previously, I was a full-time research assistant at the Research Center for IT Innovation, Academia Sinica, working with [Prof. Chuan-Ju Wang](http://cfda.csie.org/~cjwang/). I earned my master's degree in Management Information Systems from National Chengchi University, advised by Profs. Howard Hao-Chun Chuang and Yen-Chun Chou, where I worked on econometric modeling and stochastic optimization.

My recent research interests include representation learning and retrieval-augmented generation, with a focus on:

1. **Knowledge augmentation with retrieval**
   - Retrieval context compression
   - In-context prompt retrieval
   - Retrieval-augmented generation
2. **Neural information retrieval (text)**
   - Generalized retrieval: out-of-domain transfer and instruction-following
   - Unsupervised representation learning
   - Generator-augmented retrieval
3. **Interactive search**
   - Personalized conversational search

Feel free to contact me via email: j.ju[at]uva.nl. I'm open to inquiries and feedback related to my research and excited to explore potential collaborations :)
