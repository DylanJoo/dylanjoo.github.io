Hi there! I’m Jia-Huei (Dylan) Ju, a PhD student at the IRLab, University of Amsterdam, supervised by Dr. Andrew Yates since 2024. My research focuses on natural language processing and neural information retrieval.

Previously, I was a full-time research assistant at the Research Center for IT Innovation, Academia Sinica, working with Prof. Chuan-Ju Wang. I earned my master’s degree in Management Information Systems from National Chengchi University, advised by Profs. Howard Hao-Chun Chuang and Yen-Chun Chou, where I worked on econometric modeling and stochastic optimization.

My recent research interests include representation learning and retrieval-augmented generation, with a focus on:

  1. Knowledge augmentation with retrieval
    • Retrieval context compression
    • In-context prompt retrieval
    • Retrieval-augmented generation
  2. Neural information retrieval (text)
    • Generalized retrieval: out-of-domain transfer and instruction-following
    • Unsupervised representation learning
    • Generator-augmented retrieval
  3. Interactive search
    • Personalized conversational search

Feel free to contact me via email: j.ju[at]uva.nl. I’m open to inquiries and feedback related to my research and excited to explore potential collaborations :)
